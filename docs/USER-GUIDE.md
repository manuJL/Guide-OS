# User guide

This is the no-code guide — how to use GuideOS once it's running. If you're looking for how to
*install* it, that's the [quickstart](QUICKSTART.md).

## The screen

There are two halves.

On the left is the **chat**. You type or speak what you want to do, and GuideOS replies. On
the right is a **phone** you can poke at, with a top status bar and a dynamic island like a
real handset.

When a tutorial is running, a third panel appears between them: the **guided manual**. It's
the step-by-step card that tells you where to tap, and it's the thing that does the actual
teaching.

## Doing something

Type a request in the chat, like:

- "how do I send a photo on WhatsApp?"
- "set an alarm for 7"
- "turn on wifi"

You don't need exact wording. GuideOS looks for the gist of what you're asking. Press the
microphone instead if you'd rather say it out loud — more on that below.

If it recognises the task, the manual panel opens and the phone highlights your first tap,
pulsing around the button you need. Do the thing on the phone and it moves to the next step.

Not sure what to ask? Click **Give me manual** and pick a task from the menu.

## Following the steps

Each step gives you the short instruction and points at the control. Tap it, and:

- Got it right? The highlight disappears, the progress advances, and the next step lights up.
- Tapped the wrong thing? You get a small nudge telling you where to look. Nothing breaks and
  you don't lose your place.

Some steps come with a **Why this step** toggle. It explains the reasoning — helpful if you're
trying to *understand* the phone rather than just get one job done. It's off by default.

You can leave the phone in whatever state you like. If a tutorial has you open WhatsApp, you
can wander into Settings and come back; the steps still work.

## The voice

By default GuideOS reads each step aloud. There are buttons to:

- **Replay** the current step if you missed it.
- **Pause** or resume narration.
- **Mute**, which stops the voice without losing your place.

The voice follows the language you're using — an Indian English, Hindi, or Telugu voice, if
your device has one installed. If it sounds robotic or missing, that's your OS, not GuideOS;
the [voice doc](VOICE-AND-SPEECH.md) covers it.

## Language

GuideOS speaks three: **English**, **हिन्दी** (Hindi), and **తెలుగు** (Telugu). Pick one from
the language control, or just start typing in Hindi or Telugu and it switches on its own.
Your choice is remembered for next time. Anything without a translation yet falls back to
English, so you never see an empty label.

## Trying it on your own

The phone is a simulation — nothing you do in it affects a real device. Feel free to tap
around. If you want to start a tutorial over, just ask for it again or pick it from the
manual menu.

## If something isn't working

**The phone highlights nothing.** A tutorial might not be running. Ask for a task in the chat,
or use **Give me manual**.

**You tapped the right spot but nothing happened.** Give it a moment — the highlight and the
speech take a beat to catch up after you click.

**No sound.** Check the tab isn't muted and you've clicked somewhere on the page first. See
[VOICE-AND-SPEECH.md](VOICE-AND-SPEECH.md) if it persists.

**The microphone button doesn't work.** Speech recognition needs Chrome or Edge (Safari is
partial, Firefox doesn't support it). Typing always works. You'll also need to allow mic
access.
